# Community Guidelines

Our goal is to help people discover local groups and foster positive real-world connections. To keep the map safe, helpful, and welcoming for everyone, all community creators and users must follow these guidelines.

---

## 1. Respect & Courtesy

* **Be Civil:** Communities pinned on the map must be polite and welcoming to everyone.
* **No Hate Speech:** We have zero tolerance for hate speech, harassment, discrimination, bullying, or intimidation based on race, ethnicity, religion, disability, gender, age, or sexual orientation.

---

## 2. Illegal & Harmful Activities

You may **not** use our platform to link to, promote, or facilitate:
* Illegal activities, goods, or services.
* Violence, graphic content, or dangerous behavior.
* Spam, scams, malware, or fraudulent schemes.
* Explicit or sexually suggestive material.

---

## 3. Privacy & Safety (No Doxxing)

* **Protect Yourself:** Do not pin map tiles over exact personal home addresses or include personal phone numbers/private addresses in public descriptions.
* **Protect Others:** Never publish private information (doxxing) of any individual on the map without their explicit consent.

---

## 4. Review Process & Enforcement

* **Submission Approval:** All new community listings are reviewed in an administrative queue prior to appearing on the live map to prevent spam and unsafe content.
* **Takedowns:** We reserve the right to reject submissions, remove existing community map pins, or suspend associated accounts **with or without prior notice** if a listing violates these guidelines or our Terms & Conditions.

---
## 5. Illegal Content & Takedown Notices (UK Online Safety Act)

In compliance with UK online safety standards, we operate a strict notice-and-takedown system for illegal content. We strictly prohibit map listings that facilitate:

* Hate speech, harassment, or incite violent crimes.
* Fraud, illegal drugs, or unlawful activities.
* Any content or external links that pose a risk of harm to children or vulnerable individuals.

### Reporting illegal or non-compliant content
If you encounter a community pin or external link on our map that violates UK law or our guidelines, please email [removals@hexlink.org](mailto:removals@hexlink.org) with:

1. **Community Name** and map coordinates/tiles.
2. A description and evidence of the violation or illegality.

We review reported listings in accordance with our administrative queue and will remove non-compliant content promptly.
