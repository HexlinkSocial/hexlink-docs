# Privacy policy

**Last Updated:** 28 July, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our tile-map community platform.

---

## 1. Information We Collect & How It Is Used

We strictly limit the data we collect to operate the platform safely:

### A. Account & Admin Data
When you register to create a community on our map, your credentials are processed via **AWS Cognito**. We collect:
* **Email Address:** Used for account login, identity verification, and platform moderation. 
* **Password:** Securely hashed and managed via AWS Cognito.

:::note
Your email address is visible to our platform administrators in the **admin approval console** to review community submissions, combat spam, and send administrative notifications. **Your email address is never published publicly on the map.**
:::

### B. Public Map Listings
Information submitted in the "Create Community" form (Community Name, Description, selected Map Tiles, and social links) will be made publicly available to users browsing the map.

---

## 2. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

* **Third-Party Infrastructure:** We utilize Amazon Web Services (AWS) Cognito for authentication and account management.
* **No Public Exposure or Sale:** We do not publish your private contact details, nor do we sell, rent, or monetize your personal data.
---

## 3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

* **AWS Cognito:** We use Amazon Web Services Cognito to securely process and store account credentials.
* **No Selling of Data:** We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or advertisers.
* **External Links:** Clicking external links (e.g., WhatsApp, Discord) directs you to third-party platforms governed by their own privacy policies.

---

## 4. Account Deletion & Data Rights

You have the right to request deletion of your account and any community listings created under your account. To submit a request, please email us from the email associated with your AWS Cognito account to [removals@hexlink.org](mailto:removals@hexlink.org)

---

## 5. Legal Identity & UK Data Rights (UK GDPR)

This platform is operated independently by an individual developer ("Data Controller") based in the United Kingdom.

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), you have the right to access, update, or request the deletion of your personal data (such as your account email).

### Contacting Us
For any privacy inquiries, data deletion requests, or formal notifications, please contact us directly via email at [general@hexlink.org](mailto:general@hexlink.com)

If you are unsatisfied with how your data is handled, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK data protection authority:
