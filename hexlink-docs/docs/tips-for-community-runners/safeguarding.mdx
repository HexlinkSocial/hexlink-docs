# Safeguarding

One responsibility as a runner you do take on besides just being a cool person who made something is looking out for others.

## 1. Harassment & Abuse Management

If someone in your group is behaving in a way that violates our [Community Guidelines](/docs/governance/community-guidelines) (e.g., harassing others, using hate speech, or making people uncomfortable):

* **Act Promptly:** Warn, mute, or ban offending members from your external platforms (WhatsApp, Discord, real-world events, etc.).
* **Document Incidents:** Keep screenshots or notes of incidents in case further moderation or legal action is required.
* **Runner Accountability:** If a community listed on Hexlink repeatedly fails to address reported harassment or dangerous behavior, we reserve the right to remove your community listing from the map and suspend your account.

---

## 2. Managing Underage Members (18+ Policy)

Hexlink is strictly an **18+ platform**. If you discover that someone in your community is under 18:

* **Remove Them From the Group:** Politely explain that your group is strictly 18+ and remove them from your chat spaces or events.
* **Do Not Hold Private Conversations:** If you need to communicate their removal, keep it brief and formal, or inform them publicly in the group before removing them.
* **Child Safety Hazards:** If an underage person discloses abuse, exploitation, or illegal activity involving adults in your group, preserve evidence (screenshots) and report it to local law enforcement or the appropriate child protection authorities immediately.

---

## 3. Handling Self-Harm & Mental Health Crises

If a member mentions self-harm, expresses suicidal thoughts, or appears to be in an immediate acute crisis (online or at an in-person event):

### Emergency / Immediate Danger
* **If in immediate physical danger:** Call local emergency services (e.g., 999 in the UK, 911 in the US/Canada, 112 in Europe). If at an in-person event, stay with the person until help arrives if safe to do so.

### Online Disclosures or Distress
1. **Stay Calm & Direct:** Do not promise absolute secrecy, but respond with care and without judgment.
2. **Provide Crisis Helpline Resources:** Direct them to free, confidential crisis support services immediately.
   * **UK:** 
     * Samaritans: Call **116 123** or email `jo@samaritans.org`
     * Shout Crisis Text Line: Text **SHOUT** to **85258**
   * **US & Canada:** Call or text **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline)
   * **International:** Direct them to [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com)
3. **Set Boundaries:** As a community runner, you are a facilitator, not a trained therapist or emergency service. Do not attempt to counsel someone through a major crisis alone—guide them toward professional help.
4. **Remove Triggering Public Content:** If someone posts graphic details or methods of self-harm in public chats, remove the messages gently to protect other members, and reach out to the individual privately with crisis helpline contacts.

---

## 4. Media & Photo Consent

When organizing in-person events or running online spaces:

* **Obtain Consent:** Always ask for explicit consent before taking photos or recording videos of members, especially before posting them publicly on social media.
* **Respect Opt-Outs:** Give attendees a simple way to opt out of media capture (e.g., letting people know before taking group photos so anyone can step out).
* **Remove Upon Request:** If a member asks for a photo featuring them to be removed from your community channels, honor that request promptly.
