# Become and ambassador

If you'd like to spread the word about us (which also helps you to meet more people!), here are a couple ways you can do it

## Social media
- Repost and engage with our social media [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@hexlink.org) [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/hexlink_org)
- If you see someone looking for exactly this, mention the organisation in the comments (you can tag us as well) 
- If you're feeling particularly passionate, feel free to create content (text, images or videos) spreading the word!

### Having fun because of us? Share it!
If you met your besties through Hexlink and have some content of you having fun (pictures, videos, funny chat screenshots):
:::warning
Please make sure you have the permission of everyone portrayed to share this
:::
- Post it yourself and tag us
- Send it to us at [hexlink.team+socials@gmail.com](mailto:hexlink.team+socials@gmail.com) so we can use it 

## Local outreach
- Tell a friend
- Put up posters on local *public* community boards - email us at [hexlink.team+materials@gmail.com](mailto:hexlink.team+materials@gmail.com) for us to send some to you
:::warning
Please DO NOT put this up anywhere unauthorized or staple it to trees
:::
- Reach out to local areas like libraries to see if they'd like to display posters or leaflets
